The words **Derbis**, the Derbis logo and icon, word marks, screenshots, artwork,
marketing images, application identity, official package names, and visual trade dress
are proprietary brand assets controlled by Yasuda (the **Derbis Marks**).

## No implied permission

A software license, public repository, official download, donation, support link, or
factual mention of Derbis does not grant any right to use the Derbis Marks.

Without Yasuda's prior written permission, you may not use the Derbis Marks to:

- name, brand, advertise, distribute, or promote any app, fork, modified build,
  derivative work, repository, service, domain, social account, or product;
- imply affiliation with, endorsement by, sponsorship from, or official status of
  Derbis or Yasuda;
- alter, crop, recolor, animate, combine, or create confusingly similar marks;
- use official screenshots, UI art, logos, app-store graphics, or marketing images;
  or
- register or seek rights in a confusingly similar name, mark, package identifier,
  domain, or account.

## Limited factual reference

A truthful, non-misleading textual reference to Derbis may be made only when necessary
to identify the official product and only if it does not use a logo, imply endorsement,
or appear more prominently than the referring party's own name.

## Official builds

Only binaries published by Yasuda through an authorized channel may be described as
“official Derbis,” use the Derbis Marks, or use an official package identity.

## Enforcement

Yasuda may require immediate removal or correction of any unauthorized or confusing
use and may pursue available trademark, copyright, unfair-competition, contract, and
takedown remedies.
