# Derbis Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Derbis, developed by Yasuda ("Derbis", "we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Android or desktop app.

## 1. Privacy approach

Derbis is designed as a local-first music player. We do not operate a Derbis account system, sell personal data, or run behavioural advertising in the app.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Derbis may store the following locally on your device:

- settings, theme choices, language choice, and playback preferences;
- local library indexes, playlists, queue state, favourites, listening history, and download records;
- artwork cache files and optional offline media files;
- optional imported playlist information;
- optional connected-service session data or tokens where a connection feature is enabled.

You can remove local data by using the app's relevant clear/remove controls or by uninstalling the app. Device backups and operating-system storage policies may retain copies outside Derbis's control.

## 3. Network requests and third parties

When you choose online features, your device may connect directly to third-party services such as music/search providers, lyric providers, GitHub Releases, Spotify, Discord, or a remote listening server that you select.

Those services may receive information necessary to process the request, such as your IP address, device/network information, search terms, requested media identifiers, and service authorization data. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Examples:

- **Online search, lyrics, and playback resolution:** sends the request needed to locate or retrieve the requested content.
- **Playlist import:** sends or reads the link/file information you choose to import.
- **Spotify connection:** uses Spotify's authorization flow only when you start it. Derbis does not ask for your Spotify password.
- **Discord Rich Presence:** if available and enabled, sends the playback status you choose to share to Discord.
- **Update checks:** contact GitHub's release API to determine whether a newer Derbis package is available.
- **Derbis Jam — LAN:** shares listening-session data only with devices on the session you join or host. Remote Jam features connect only to an endpoint you explicitly select.

## 4. Audio analysis

If Beat Align is enabled, Derbis analyses a limited local PCM playback sample on your device to estimate BPM and a beat-grid candidate. Raw audio is not uploaded for this analysis and is discarded after processing. Only a small local result needed for the feature may be cached.

## 5. No child-directed service

Derbis is not directed at children. If you are not old enough to consent under the laws that apply to you, use Derbis only with a parent or guardian's permission.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures in the app to reduce unnecessary data exposure. However, no application, local device, or network transmission is completely secure. Keep your device secure and do not share authorization tokens or private server addresses.

## 7. Your choices

You can choose not to use optional online, import, social, and remote-listening features. You may remove local playlists, downloads, history, connected-service settings, and app data from your device.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy as Derbis changes. The current version is published at this URL with its effective date.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the official [Derbis GitHub repository](https://github.com/jusnie/Derbis/issues).
