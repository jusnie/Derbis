Capabilities

Built for people who keep files and stream too

Everything listed here ships in the current Android APK.

Apple Music 13, Pixel UI, and five more player decks

Choose how the Now Playing screen works: Apple Music 13 with word-timed inline lyrics and a locked audio tier chip, the blush-paper Pixel UI skin with 0 dp square chrome across every screen and exported receipt, or the 3D Vinyl queue stage with smooth 90/120 Hz Spotify Canvas loops.

Pixel UI Apple Music 13 Flow Sleeve Late Night Frost Derbis One Vinyl 3D stage

Discord Rich Presence & scrobbling

Publishes your active song, real progress timestamps, and the current synced lyric line directly to Discord over the mobile Social SDK flow, alongside Last.fm and ListenBrainz scrobbling.

Personal server integration

Connect Navidrome, Gonic, or Airsonic to search and play your own FLAC, ALAC, and MP3 files untranscoded next to streamed songs.

24-bit audio chain & DSP

Parametric EQ with AutoEQ import, ReplayGain, crossfade, vocal-canceling karaoke, mono output, and a live signal sheet.

Rule-based offline storage

Resumable downloads with Wi-Fi and charging rules, plus a bounded cache that trims old tracks before storage fills up.

Derbis Jam rooms

Start a real-time Listen Together session with a shareable room code so friends hear the same queue in sync.

Changelog

Every release and what changed

Synced with CHANGELOG.md in the repository, with live per-release APK counters from GitHub.

v1.8.0 30 October 2026 — downloads 10 added · 5 fixed

Added 10 total

  • Pixel UI skin — blush paper ground, plum ink, 0 dp square chrome across surfaces and modals, and bundled Pixelify display type.
  • Vinyl queue stage — the live queue arranged as a 3D sleeve row turning on a single shared arc.
  • Subsonic & Navidrome — connect your personal server and search FLAC, ALAC, and MP3 files alongside streamed songs.
  • Spotify Canvas multi-tier & SimpleCache — Pathfinder and regional spclient resolution with disk pre-warming and 20s RAM retention.
  • Mono output, Artwork ceilings (480/720/1080 px), Status bar lyrics & Explicit filter.

Fixed 5 total

  • Spotify Canvas sign-in stays on accounts.spotify.com with a Chrome UA, captures sp_dc, and refreshes session caches immediately.
  • Canvas playback keeps 90/120 Hz LTPO displays at full refresh rate and moves dynamic palette sampling off the UI thread.
  • Stream playback recovers from cellular STATE_IDLE stalls, REEL_AUDIO probe misclassifications, and PCM16 sink stalls; MonoAudioProcessor flushes full buffers.
  • Discord Rich Presence publishes full progress timestamps and synced lyrics over the Social SDK path.
  • Wrapped receipt export adds 720 px and 540 px fallbacks, and the player audio codec badge hugs its exact label width.

v1.5.0 2 November 2026 — downloads 17 added · 24 changed · 9 fixed

Added 17 total

  • Derbis One and Frost — a paper deck and a glass deck, each drawing its own seek control.
  • Karaoke — real-time vocal center cancellation that preserves low-end kick and bass.
  • The Derbis Notch — a capsule above the app that opens smoothly into the player.
  • Lossless output — 24-bit float audio path on wired, USB, HDMI, and lossless Bluetooth codecs.
  • Wrapped receipts can be exported from any page as a 1080 px PNG.

Changed 24 total

  • Liquid Glass moved to a fixed recipe without manual blur, tint, and refraction sliders.
  • Streaming quality settled on four steps: Low, Medium, High, Lossless.
  • The player badge reports audible quality — LOSSLESS, HI-FI, AAC — rather than container format.
  • Liner Notes and Folio were retired and migrate saved preferences cleanly to Sleeve.

Fixed 9 total

  • Discord Connect reaches the installed Discord app directly without failing on Android 11+ resolveActivity() checks.
  • Discord sign-in uses the mobile callback scheme discord-<id>:/authorize/callback.
  • Karaoke word timing and Liquid Glass layer sampling were corrected.

v1.1.0 27 October 2026 — downloads 13 added · 9 changed · 12 fixed

Added 13 total

  • Apple Music 13 deck with word-timed lyrics and the Tidal Music palette.
  • Endless Listening for single-song sessions without altering album queues.
  • Opt-in Canvas artwork catalog and provider-flagged AI content filtering.

Changed 9 total

  • Derbis relicensed to GPL-3.0 so anyone can inspect, rebuild, and share it.

Fixed 12 total

  • Paused tracks keep full sleeve scale, recently played songs restart cleanly from 0:00, and placeholder genre stickers were removed.
