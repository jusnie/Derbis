What it does

Things you would notice
in the first ten minutes.

Everything below is in the app today, not a roadmap.

Seven player decks & Canvas

Flow, Sleeve, Apple Music 13, Late Night, Frost, Derbis One and Vinyl — where the live queue turns through a 3D sleeve stage — plus smooth Spotify Canvas loops.

Pixel skin

A blush-paper skin with hard corners and one bundled pixel typeface — on every screen, and on the images it exports.

Your own library

Point it at Navidrome, Gonic or Airsonic and your files play next to the streams — originally, untranscoded, in the same search.

Discord & Last.fm

Rich Presence with the lyric line and a real progress bar, plus scrobbling to Last.fm and ListenBrainz.

Sound you control

Parametric EQ with AutoEQ import, ReplayGain, crossfade, karaoke, 8D, mono output, and a technical sheet that says what is running.

Offline that works

Downloads with Wi-Fi and charging rules, resumable files, and a smart queue that decides what to keep before you run out of space.

v1.8.0 21 November 2026 — downloads 10 new5 fixed

Added 10 in total, 5 shown

  • Pixel skin — blush paper ground, plum ink, 0 dp rectangular chrome across every surface and modal, and monospace headings.
  • Vinyl queue stage — the live queue arranged as a 3D cover-flow row turning on a single shared arc.
  • Subsonic & Navidrome — connect your home server and search your personal FLAC/ALAC/MP3 library alongside streamed music.
  • Spotify Canvas multi-tier & SimpleCache — Pathfinder, regional spclient protobuf and fallback resolution with disk pre-warming and 20s RAM loop retention.
  • Mono output, Artwork ceilings (480/720/1080 px), Status bar lyrics & Explicit filter.

Fixed 5 in total, 5 shown

  • Spotify Canvas in-app sign-in stays on accounts.spotify.com with a Chrome UA, captures sp_dc cleanly and refreshes session caches immediately.
  • Canvas playback keeps 90/120 Hz LTPO displays at full refresh rate and moves dynamic palette sampling off the UI thread.
  • Stream playback recovers from cellular STATE_IDLE stalls, REEL_AUDIO probe misclassifications and PCM16 sink stalls; MonoAudioProcessor flushes full buffers.
  • Discord Rich Presence publishes full progress timestamps and synced lyrics over the Social SDK path.
  • Wrapped receipt export adds 720 px and 540 px fallbacks, and the player audio codec badge hugs its exact label width.

v1.5.0 2 November 2026 — downloads 17 new24 changed9 fixed

Added 17 in total, 5 shown

  • Derbis One and Frost — a paper deck and a glass deck, each drawing its own seek control instead of borrowing the shared bar.
  • Karaoke — a real-time processor that cancels the centre of the mix and keeps the low band, so the kick survives.
  • The Derbis Notch — a capsule above the app that opens into the player, morphing on one spring instead of swapping.
  • Lossless output — a 24-bit float path on wired, USB, HDMI and the lossless Bluetooth codecs; 16-bit everywhere else, on purpose.
  • Wrapped receipts can be shared from any page as a 1080 px PNG, not only at the finale.

Changed 24 in total, 5 shown

  • Liquid Glass became a fixed, Apple-matched recipe — the tuning panel and every blur, tint and refraction slider are gone.
  • Streaming quality settled on four steps: Low, Medium, High, Lossless. Auto was removed.
  • The player badge reports what you hear — LOSSLESS, HI-FI, AAC — instead of the container carrying it.
  • Liner Notes and Folio were retired; a saved choice now lands on Sleeve rather than a blank player.
  • Beat Sync left Settings entirely, because it could only align tracks already played. Mix Studio keeps it per transition, where the pair is known.

Fixed 9 in total, 5 shown

  • Discord Connect reaches the Discord app. It used to ask `resolveActivity()` first, which answers null on Android 11+ and sent every attempt to a browser tab.
  • Discord sign-in uses the redirect Discord actually accepts on mobile, `discord-:/authorize/callback`.
  • Liquid Glass draws glass instead of a flat film — the recorded page layer wrapped the very bars that had to sample it.
  • Karaoke follows the vocal word by word after three stacked timing faults were untangled.
  • The glass GPU guard waits for two consecutive foreground deaths before pausing the material, and ignores a marker from another build.

v1.1.0 27 October 2026 — downloads 13 new9 changed12 fixed2 removed

Added 13 in total, 4 shown

  • The Apple Music 13 deck with its dedicated word-timed lyric view, and the Tidal Music deck with its own palette.
  • Endless Listening for single-song sessions, without leaking into albums and playlists.
  • Opt-in Canvas artwork through a self-hosted catalogue instead of a public relay.
  • The AI Slop setting, which blocks content a provider has explicitly flagged as AI-generated.

Changed 9 in total, 4 shown

  • Derbis became GPL-3.0, replacing the custom end-user agreement, so anyone can rebuild and share it.
  • The Windows desktop client was retired — its folder, Gradle build, workflow and docs are gone.
  • Videos were removed: Derbis is songs only.
  • Full-player gestures — tap, long-press, double-tap-to-seek, vertical swipe — were removed rather than left half-working.

Fixed 12 in total, 4 shown

  • Pausing a song no longer shrinks the player cover.
  • A recently played track starts from the beginning instead of resuming an old position.
  • Album and discovery artwork no longer fall back to initials, genre stickers or decorative symbols.
  • The Liquid Glass guard no longer punishes a normal exit after a single process death.

v1.0.3 25 October 2026 — downloads 12 Android7 Windows

Android 12 entries

  • Mix Studio gained saved, playlist-specific transition plans, Auto Mix and editable handoff modes.
  • Beat Align (beta) analysed local BPM and beat grids, and refused to align where it was not confident.
  • Imported playlists are labelled Imported from Spotify instead of looking native.
  • Release packaging was fixed so the APK actually builds.

Windows 7 entries

  • The first and only native Windows release, with MSI and EXE installers, a Start-menu shortcut and `derbis://` link handling.
  • The installers were never code-signed, so SmartScreen asked for confirmation.

Note historical

  • This is the release that introduced the Windows client, retired two days later in 1.1.0.
  • Its Android half shipped Mix Studio and the Jam groundwork the later versions built on.
