Brand logos on the sign-in buttons

The Discord and Spotify rows in Settings → Integrations currently use a generic Material account glyph. They get the real brand marks instead — CC0 path data from simple-icons, drawn as vector drawables, no bitmaps. Under Pixel the tiles keep their skin (paper card, band plate, square corners) and only the mark keeps its own colour, because a brand colour is not the skin's to repaint.

3 — Are these two screens affected by Pixel today?

SurfaceFollows Pixel?Why
Settings tile shell (card fill, plate shape, plate fill, icon tint, chevron) Yes Goes through SkinChrome: plate becomes PixelDesign.controlCorner = 0 dp, fills with the band tone, type turns plum. Also uppercases labels.
The icon inside the tile Yes, wrongly Every settings icon is a Material glyph tinted with the skin accent, so "Connect Discord" draws a rose account circle. That is the thing this change fixes — the mark keeps its brand colour instead.
Discord connected profile card No Hardcoded #16181F fill, #262835 border, #2C2E38 avatar well, white text. Stays Discord-dark on every skin, including blush paper.
Spotify card No Hardcoded #1ED760 green, Color.White at 4 %/8 %/10 %, 18 dp corners. On Pixel it is a dark rounded card sitting in a paper list.

So: the tile rows are already pixel-shaped, but the two expanded cards are not. Those hardcoded colours are the leftover — the same class of bug as the share cards. Both have been brought onto the palette; the brand accents (Discord blurple, Spotify green) stay as the only fixed hues.
