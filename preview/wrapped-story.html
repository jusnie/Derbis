Wrapped — the whole story, nine slides

Pixel skin applied. The switch at the bottom right turns the skin off and redraws all nine slides on the default skin — same deck, same geometry, different colours, corners and type. That is the rule the app follows: Pixel applies only while it is equipped.

The story has always been nine pages, but the preview only ever drew the header of one of them. Here is the deck as it actually runs, in order, on the Pixel skin: Intro, Minutes, Artist, Tracks, Albums, Rhythm, Day, Persona, Finale. Every slide keeps the same skeleton — an 18 % band under the status bar, a 16 % band at the foot, 2 dp rules in edge, a rose tick — and only the middle changes.

The skin does not recolor the story per slide. Nine pages on one ground is the point: the deck reads as one object when you swipe it, which is what a wrapped story is for. What changes page to page is which data is on it, never which palette.

2 — The header, which is what used to break

Two circles, a pill and a title, on one row. The bug was geometry: Material's IconButton reserves a 48 dp touch target around a 36 dp circle, so the pair took 96 dp of row. The circles are now drawn at 36 dp inside explicit 48 dp slots, the two actions share one group with a fixed 8 dp between them, and the pill is the only child that gives way.

Header row — final geometry

Close and Share in 48 dp slots, 8 dp apart, pinned to the leading and trailing edge. The period pill (e.g. LAST 365 DAYS) sits between them and is the only flexible child: it ellipsises before either circle moves. At a large font size the row stays 56 dp tall and the circles do not drift off the edge.

Tapping Share renders the receipt at 1080 px and falls back to 720 then 540 if the device cannot hold the bitmap — the story screen already has the covers and the backdrop in memory, so a phone that cannot allocate the full card gets a smaller one instead of the “couldn't create the image” line.

What the three states share

  • Same card. Running, empty and complete are one component with different copy — not three screens.
  • The picker is always lit. A tapped year that has data always opens; a year with none says so.
  • No zero slide. A story that would draw 0 minutes is never shown.

4 — What the skin does here, and what it does not

SurfacePixelEvery other skin
Ground Blush paper #FFEDF1, band #FFD9E2. Its own ground, unchanged.
Corners 0 dp — every slide is a hard rectangle. As it shipped.
Type Pixelify Sans, the same face as the rest of the app. Its own face.
Accent Rose #9E1B44 rules, hot #FF5C8A for the one figure that matters. The artwork's own palette.
Cover art Drawn as-is. No tint, no duotone, no blend. Drawn as-is.
Layout Identical in every skin. The skin changes colour, corners and type — never a slide's structure or the number of pages.
