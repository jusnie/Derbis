Cover Flow — what replaces the Vinyl deck

The stage is the queue. The current track sits forward and flat; the two on either side are turned away on the Y axis, scaled down and dimmed, each showing its own title. Pressing next or previous does not swap two pictures — the whole row turns: every card slides and rotates through the arc at once, and the one that lands in the centre straightens up.

The turn

  • Arc each card's distance d from the playhead sets its transform: translateX(d × 0.58 × card), rotateY(−d × 52°), scale(1 − |d| × 0.12), scrim |d| × 0.26. The row is one animated float, not a swap.
  • Turn next and previous animate that float by ±1 over 420 ms with FastOutSlowInEasing; the outgoing card keeps turning as it leaves, so the motion reads as a wheel, not a cross-fade.
  • Snap with animations off the float jumps in one frame — same rule the Vinyl deck already follows.
  • Depth cameraDistance = 14 × density, raised from the default so a 52° turn does not fan the card into a trapezoid.

Drive it

What it takes in the app

  • Deck the deck API hands a deck one Track. This needs the queue: PlayerDeckQueue(previous, current, next, index, total) added to PlayerDeckSlots, filled from state.queue and state.queueCurrentIndex — both already in scope in DerbisNowPlaying.
  • Direction rememberTrackStepDirection() already returns ±1 for a skip; it becomes the sign of the turn, so a native previous turns the other way.
  • Rename PlayerVisualMode.Vinyl → CoverFlow and PlayerDeckLayout.Vinyl → CoverFlow, with "vinyl" kept in PlayerVisualMode.from() so anyone who picked Vinyl lands on the new deck rather than on the fallback.
  • Files PlayerDeckVinyl.kt becomes PlayerDeckCoverFlow.kt; the panel, seek, transport and lyric strip carry over unchanged. The title moves onto the card's plate, so the separate headline goes.
  • Art neighbours use the same CoverImage slot the centre card uses; no extra loading path, no image assets.
