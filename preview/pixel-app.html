Pixel — the app screens

What the skin looks like on the surfaces you actually move through: home, library, search and the settings hub. Everything here is drawn from the shipped palette — blush paper ground, cream cards, band panels, plum ink, a rose accent that clears 4.5:1 on every one of them, no rounded corners anywhere, and every type role set in Pixelify Sans, an OFL pixel face bundled as two 50 KB instances because the F-Droid build has no Play Services to download one.

Pixel · shipped in code
Skinned in bac1fb6 — palette, geometry and type all come from one place.

4 — What the skin does and does not touch

ThingPixel
Ground, cards, panelsPaper #FFEDF1, cream #FFF8F6, band #FFD9E2 / #FFC9D6
InkPlum #5B2338, muted #7E3B57 — 10.7:1 and 7.0:1 on paper
AccentRose #9E1B44 — 6.9:1 on paper, 6.0:1 on the deepest band
Corners0 dp everywhere: cards, sheets, buttons, dialogs, artwork, mini player
TypePixelify Sans, all fifteen roles. Two bundled instances, 103 KB; kana and kanji fall back to the system face.
Artwork, album covers, brand marksNot touched. A cover keeps its colour; a brand keeps its hue.
Other skinsUntouched. Every branch reads the equipped skin and never runs otherwise.
