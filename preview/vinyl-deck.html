What moves, and why

  • Sleeve the artwork itself, square, 3 dp corner — the stage slot every deck already gets.
  • Disc slides out 46 % of its width while playing, glides back when paused. Rotation is positionMs × 0.2° — a 33⅓ rpm disc, so scrubbing turns it to the same place every time.
  • Label a conic gradient pressed from the artwork's two accents, with the spindle hole. It rotates with the disc.
  • Label the record's centre is the only progress cue on the disc: the playhead is the bar underneath, so nothing competes with the artwork.
  • Motion every animation is a tween on animated and snaps when animations are off (item 66's rule, and the notch's).
