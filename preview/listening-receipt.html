Listening receipt — three styles, and what Pixel does to them

Pixel skin applied. This page always shows both receipts, because the comparison is the point. The switch at the bottom right decides which one is marked equipped, and dims the other one.

The receipt is the 4:5 card you share at the end of the story. It has three styles, and until now only one of them had ever been drawn in a preview. All three are here, side by side with what they look like on the default skin, because the interesting part is what the skin changes: Noir is pure black on the default skin and blush paper on Pixel.

That is not a bug and it is not a compromise. The skin's own steps are the three styles now — Aurora is blush paper with a rose accent, Noir is the deeper band, Paper is the cream sheet — so the chooser still does something without inventing a fourth style and a new translated label for it.

1 — Pixel, all three styles

Same content, same layout, same 4:5 export. Only the ground and the two accents move. Every card is a hard rectangle: corner = 0, no glow, no gradient number.

The difference · per style

  • Ground — paper to band. The default card follows the artwork; this one does not.
  • Accent — rose #9E1B44 with hot #FF5C8A for the one figure that leads.
  • Corners — 0 dp. The default keeps its rounded card.
  • Tiles — square, rose at 16 %. No rounded chips.

The one thing to decide

On the default skin the three styles are genuinely different moods — a coloured gradient, a black card, a cream card. On Pixel all three are light, because a blush-paper skin has no black step to give Noir without breaking the skin. They still differ (deeper band, different accent order), but the contrast between them is smaller than it is elsewhere.

That is why the code comment says the chooser “still does something” rather than “works exactly as before”. If you would rather Noir stay dark on Pixel — a deliberate near-black card inside a blush skin — that is a one-line change to its two ground stops, and this page is the place to judge it.

3 — What the export guarantees

BehaviourDetail
Size 1080 px wide at 4:5, falling back to 720 then 540 if the bitmap cannot be allocated.
Content Minutes, artist and track counts, the top three tracks, and the 24-hour rhythm strip.
Rendering Drawn, not screenshotted. Hardware bitmaps are disabled for the export, so the result is identical on every GPU.
Style Whichever chip is selected. The choice is remembered for the session, not written to settings.
Failure Each stage that can fail — cache dir, draw, file, provider URI, chooser — reports itself instead of a single “couldn't create the image”.

Honest limit. These are drawn mocks at the real 4:5 geometry, not screenshots. The figures are invented so the layout is exercised at realistic lengths. The hexes are read from the skin's receipt theme in the source, so the palette here is the palette that ships.
