Pixel

A pink pixel skin that replaces Nocturne 78. Same skeleton as Nocturne 78 — one palette, one chrome pass, no per-screen forks — but every surface is hard-edged: 2 dp borders, no corner radius, no blur, no glass, offset drop shadows in a single rose ink, and a dithered fill for anything that fills. Pick a card, a bow, or a heart from the shelf and it lands on the mini player as a ribbon.

The rules it follows

Corners0 dp everywhere. Every surface is a rectangle with a 2 dp rose border.
ShadowOffset only — 2 to 4 dp, hard, no blur. No elevation, no glow, no gradient falloff.
FillsAnything that fills (progress, badges, active tab) is either flat hot pink or a 4 dp checker dither.
TypeMonospace, uppercase, wide tracking. No bundled font — the system mono keeps the app size unchanged.
MotionSteps, not tweens: page and sheet transitions land in 2 frames; nothing eases. Still obeys the animations switch.
No glassPixel opts out of the Liquid Glass film entirely. Fresh, not frosted.

Scope, honestly

Removal: `DerbisSkin.Nocturne78` becomes `Pixel`, and the persisted string `"Nocturne78"` maps to `Pixel` in `DerbisSkin.from()` — the same way `"Prism"` already maps to Liquid Glass, so nobody's saved choice resets to Default. The palette file keeps its shape, the 123 references follow the rename.
Chrome: home header, shelves and tiles, bottom nav, mini player, settings rows, sheets and dialogs get the pixel pass — the pieces you see above, not a per-screen fork.
Decks: the seven player decks are artwork-coloured by design; under Pixel the frame, transport and seek turn to the pixel language but the artwork still drives the colour, which is what keeps them from fighting the skin.
Originality: bow, heart and star are drawn as pixel sprites in this repo — CSS and inline SVG only, no image files, no bundled character, nothing borrowed.
