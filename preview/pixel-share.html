Pixel on every surface the app can hand out

Item 101. Until now the pixel skin stopped at the screen edge: the lyric page dimmed the album cover and wrote in white, the lyric share image used the artwork's darkened palette, the track story card bloomed the cover's colours behind white type, the receipt read the artwork palette and the wrapped story sat on near-black. Every one of those is now the same paper the app draws on: blush ground, band panels, plum ink, rose accent, 2 dp rules, zero corner radius. Nothing bundled, nothing generated — the mocks below are CSS.
