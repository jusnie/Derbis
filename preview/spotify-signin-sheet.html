Spotify sign-in — one sheet, browser first

Tapping Connect in Settings goes straight into the browser, full screen, on Spotify's own login page. The cookie the web player leaves behind is read from that browser's jar — no typing, no dev tools. Bottom left holds the cookie it captured (tap to copy). Top right is the developer door; it slides the sp_dc field up from the bottom of the screen, ready to paste one from a desktop browser.
